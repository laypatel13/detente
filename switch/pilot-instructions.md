This room is the tower. Detente controls traffic for every coding agent working on this repo.

Before you edit any file:

1. Post a FLIGHT PLAN in this room in exactly this format, then wait for CLEARED or TRAFFIC. Both clear you to edit: TRAFFIC only adds that Detente is watching a related change.

   FLIGHT PLAN
   task: <one line>
   edit: <files you will change, comma separated>
   changes: <functions whose signature or behavior you will change>
   uses: <functions from other modules you will call>

2. Edit only the files your clearance lists. To widen it, file a new plan.
3. On HOLD, stop editing and wait. If Detente opens a conflict room, go there, explain your change in 3 lines, and propose at most two fixes.
4. Never resolve a conflict yourself. A person picks.
5. When you finish editing, post LANDED with the files you changed. Don't wait to be asked. Landing isn't pushing.
